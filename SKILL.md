# Grilling

1. Map the requested plan into decisions and their prerequisites. Reuse decisions already settled; keep unknowns explicit.
2. Ask one short round of the decisions that most constrain the next step. Number questions; give distinct options and a recommendation. Do not ask downstream questions whose inputs remain unsettled.
3. Wait for answers. A bare "yes" settles only an unambiguous decision. Update the decision tree, then ask the next necessary round.
4. Find environmental facts yourself. Use an available independent sub-agent only when useful; otherwise inspect directly. While a fact is pending, ask only questions that do not depend on it. For host delegation tools, read your agent runtime's delegation guidance.
5. Finish when decisions needed for this scope are settled and remaining assumptions/open questions are visible. Respect stop/narrow-scope requests. Summarize the agreed plan; do not implement it until the user confirms shared understanding or directly authorizes implementation.

Format each question as: **Q1 — decision:** options, then **Recommended:** choice and one reason. Keep the round short enough to answer without rereading a long questionnaire.
